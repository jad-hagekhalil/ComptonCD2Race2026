What stands out

Two electorates

Perry won in-person ballots by 211 votes. Tinson won mail ballots by 29. Perry won the vote-center vote in all seven precincts, while Tinson won the mail vote in three.

East vs. west

Tinson's three precincts (1450017A, 1450149A, 1450055A) sit on the east and north side of the district. Perry's base is the central core: 1450006A, 1450013A and 1450008A.

Perry's renters

Perry's in-person margin was largest in lower-income, renter-heavy precincts (r ≈ −0.83 with median income and −0.88 with homeownership). With only seven precincts, treat this as a strong lead, not a proof.

Late ballots

Late mail ballots are the only thing that can move this race, and so far mail has favored Tinson by just 1.9 points. Watch 1450017A, where he won mail ballots by 63 votes.

Sources and method

Results come from the LA County Registrar-Recorder's precinct report for contest cc10769 (Compton City Council District 2), generated Nov 4, 2026. "In-person" is the report's Polling Place row and "Mail" is its VBM Portion row.

Precinct boundaries come from the Registrar's election-precinct layer on the LA County GIS portal, lightly simplified for the web. Compton precincts outside District 2 are drawn in gray for context.

Demographics are American Community Survey 5-year tract estimates (Esri Living Atlas): race and ethnicity (B03002), median household income (B19049) and owner vs. renter households (B25091/B25070). Each election precinct takes the average profile of the Registrar base precincts inside it, using each base precinct's 2020 census tract. These are neighborhood estimates. Precincts in the same tract look alike, and they describe residents rather than voters.

Correlations (r) are weighted by registered voters. With 5 to 25 precincts, they show patterns worth testing against the voter file, not causes.
